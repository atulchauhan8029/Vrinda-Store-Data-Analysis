# 🛍️ Vrinda Store – 2022 Sales Data Analysis

An Excel-based data analysis and interactive dashboard to understand
customer behavior and recommend how to improve Vrinda Store's sales.

##  Problem Statement
Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022 and
identify which customers and channels to target to grow sales in 2023.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Data Cleaning)

##  Dataset
- File: `data/Vrinda_Store_Data_Analysis.xlsx`
- Contains order details: Order ID, Date, Status, Channel, Category,
  Gender, Age Group, State, Amount, Qty, etc.

##  Questions Answered
1. Compare sales and orders using a single chart
2. Which month had the highest sales and orders?
3. Who purchased more – men or women – in 2022?
4. What are the different order statuses in 2022?
5. Top 10 states contributing to sales
6. Relation between age and gender based on number of orders
7. Which channel contributes the most to sales?
8. Highest-selling category

##  Key Insights
- Women are more likely to buy than men (~65%)
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states (~35%)
- Adults aged 30–49 contribute the most (~50%)
- Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra contribute ~80% of sales

## Final Conclusion
Target **women aged 30–49** living in **Maharashtra, Karnataka and
Uttar Pradesh** with ads, offers and coupons on **Amazon, Flipkart
and Myntra**.
